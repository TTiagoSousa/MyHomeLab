v1.14.0 — More body measurements, one measurement per weigh-in and a Devices page

### Features
- **More body measurements**: BMI, fat mass, muscle percentage, skeletal muscle mass, bone mass percentage, water mass and protein mass join the others, 17 in total from the scale, each with its own page to log, averages, a chart and the history. BMI is worked out from your weight and height when the scale does not give it.
- **One measurement**: each weigh-in is a measurement. On a measurement's page you can start a new one or add the value to a measurement you already made. In Weight, press a measurement's date to see all its values.
- **Quick forms as a list**: when creating a form, choose a grid or a list; in the list you set the order of the fields.
- **Devices page**: a page just for your measuring machines (scale, blood pressure monitor, glucometer, thermometer, oximeter, other) with brand and model, opened from the Devices button on Health. Each type has a default. Readings (blood pressure, blood sugar, temperature) now also save the device.
