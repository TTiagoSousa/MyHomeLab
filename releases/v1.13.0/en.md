v1.13.0 — Body composition, quick forms and a tidier Health

### Features
- **A page for every body measurement**: body fat, subcutaneous fat, visceral fat, muscle mass, skeletal muscle, bone mass, fat-free weight, BMR, body water and protein, plus waist, chest, arm, thigh, hips and neck. Each page lets you log the value right there, and shows the averages, a chart and the history (where you can delete a single value).
- **Weigh-ins with body composition**: the Weight page now takes the weight and the 10 scale measurements in one go. Fat-free weight is worked out when your scale does not give it. Your body fat and muscle mass from Body moved here.
- **Devices**: add your scales (for example "Renpho") in Weight → Devices and pick a default; it is already chosen when you log.
- **Choose the average tabs**: on the Health page, the ⚙ button picks which tabs show (saved in your account). When they do not fit, arrows let you scroll through them. The card keeps the same size on every tab and shows your latest entries.
- **Quick forms**: in Add health data → Manual, create forms with the fields you always enter (scale, tape measurements, water) and fill them in one go. Enter moves to the next field.
- **New pages**: Conditions and allergies (moved out of Profile, which now only has your details) and Readings (blood pressure, blood sugar and temperature, moved out of Body).
- **Today**: new shortcuts for a Health form and for coffee and alcohol, both open right on Today.

### Fixes
- **Today shortcuts**: the type buttons no longer jump when you switch type, and accounts of the same app show their name or email (for example each Gmail address) instead of the app name repeated.
