v1.19.0 — Daily cron jobs at the time you choose

### Changes
- **Pick the time**: when a cron job runs "Every day", a time field shows up next to it. It runs every day at that time (Lisbon time), and the first run after turning it on is at that time too. Leave it empty and it runs 24 hours after the last run, as before. It works for every cron job: AdGuard lists, the disk alert and the updates.
- **Also through the assistant**: you can ask it to turn on an updates cron job every day at a given time.
