v1.12.0 — Weather and shortcuts on Today

### Features
- **Weather on Today**: the Today page now shows the weather at the top: the current temperature and sky, today's high, low and chance of rain, and the next 4 days. Choose your city in Settings → Weather. The data comes from Open-Meteo; only the city name and its coordinates are sent.
- **Shortcuts on Today**: add shortcuts below the weather with the **Add** button: a connected app (opens its page), the email rules, or any link (opens in a new tab). Remove one with the **×**. Shortcuts are saved in your account.
